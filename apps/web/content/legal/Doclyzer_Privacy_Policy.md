Doclyzer is a digital platform designed to help users upload, organize, review, understand, track and securely share diagnostic and laboratory reports.

## 1. Our role

When Doclyzer determines why and how your personal data is processed, Doclyzer acts as the entity responsible for that processing.

Where applicable under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act"), Doclyzer may act as a **Data Fiduciary** and you may be a **Data Principal**.

Certain service providers that process data only on our instructions may act as data processors or service providers on our behalf.

## 2. Information we collect

The information we collect depends on how you use Doclyzer.

### Account information

When you register or sign in, we may collect your mobile number, email address, name, authentication information, OTP-verification information and account identifiers.

We do not need your account password where authentication is performed through OTP or another passwordless authentication method.

### Profile information

You may provide information such as your name, date of birth or age, gender where necessary for report context, profile photograph, relationship information and other information required to organize reports.

You may also create profiles for family members.

### Family member information

If you create or manage a profile for another person, we may process that person's name, relationship, age or date of birth, gender where relevant, photograph and medical-report information.

By adding another person's information, you confirm that you are authorized to provide and manage that information.

Where the profile relates to a child, you must be the child's parent or lawful guardian or otherwise have lawful authority to manage the child's information.

### Medical reports and health information

When you upload a PDF, photograph, scan or other supported medical document, we may process the document and information contained within it.

This may include patient name, laboratory or diagnostic-provider name, report date, test names, test values, units, reference intervals, flags appearing on the report and other information contained in the uploaded document.

Medical records, health information and information derived from those records are treated with a high degree of confidentiality.

### Extracted report data

Doclyzer uses document-processing technology to extract information from uploaded reports.

Extracted information may include test names, values, measurement units, reference intervals, dates and report categories.

Because automated extraction can sometimes be incorrect, Doclyzer may ask you to review extracted information against your original report.

The original report remains an important source of verification.

### AI-generated explanations

Doclyzer may use artificial-intelligence services to generate plain-language educational explanations of test results and report information.

For this purpose, Doclyzer may process structured information derived from your report, such as test names, values, units, reference intervals and limited relevant context.

We aim to send only the information reasonably required to provide the requested explanation.

Doclyzer currently uses external AI infrastructure, including the OpenAI API, where applicable, to generate certain explanations.

Doclyzer does not use AI-generated explanations as a substitute for the original laboratory report or for professional medical advice.

### Historical and trend information

Where you have uploaded multiple reports, Doclyzer may organize results over time to generate charts, comparisons and historical views.

This processing may include matching the same or similar biomarkers across different reports and dates.

### Sharing information

If you choose to share a report, selected test result, historical trend or profile information, we may process information required to create and manage that share.

This may include the recipient's email address or phone number where applicable, the information selected for sharing, share-link identifiers, expiry information and security/access logs.

The recipient receives only the information you choose to share through the relevant sharing mechanism, subject to the product's technical operation.

### Payment and credit information

Doclyzer may offer paid credits, token packages, subscriptions or other paid functionality.

We may maintain information about purchased credits, promotional credits, referral credits, transaction identifiers, order status, refunds, chargebacks and credit usage.

Payment-card, UPI, banking or similar payment credentials may be collected directly by our payment processor, app store or payment-service provider.

Unless specifically stated otherwise, Doclyzer does not itself store complete payment-card details.

### Referral information

If you use our referral programme, we may process your referral code, the referring user's account identifier, the referred user's account identifier, referral qualification status, rewards issued and information necessary to detect fraudulent or duplicate referrals.

We do not provide the referring user with the referred user's medical-report information.

### Technical and usage information

We may automatically receive limited technical information such as device type, operating-system version, app version, IP address, timestamps, crash logs, diagnostic information, security events and request logs.

This information is used for security, troubleshooting, fraud prevention and service reliability.

### Communications

If you contact customer support, privacy support or our grievance team, we may retain the correspondence and information necessary to resolve your request.

## 3. Information we do not need for ordinary use

Unless a particular feature expressly requires it and you are informed at that time, Doclyzer does not need unrestricted access to your contacts, microphone, call history or precise location merely to analyze a medical report.

Permissions requested by the application should be limited to the functionality for which they are required.

For example, camera or file access may be requested when you choose to photograph or upload a report.

## 4. Why we process personal data

We process personal data to provide and operate Doclyzer.

This includes authenticating users, maintaining accounts and family profiles, uploading and storing reports, extracting report information, generating explanations requested by users, presenting test values, creating trends, comparing historical reports, enabling secure sharing, maintaining credits and referral rewards, processing purchases, providing customer support, detecting fraud, protecting accounts, debugging technical problems, complying with legal obligations and improving the reliability and usability of the Service.

We do not use your medical reports for targeted advertising.

We do not sell your medical reports to advertisers, insurers, employers, laboratories, data brokers or other third parties.

## 5. Consent and lawful processing

Where consent is required, Doclyzer seeks consent that is appropriate to the purpose for which the information is collected.

Sensitive health information should be collected only for purposes connected with providing Doclyzer's services.

Where the DPDP Act applies, consent-based processing will be structured to be free, specific, informed, unconditional and unambiguous and given through a clear affirmative action.

Doclyzer may present additional notices at the point where information is collected. For example, separate notices may be presented before processing a report using an external AI provider, enabling optional marketing communication or sharing a report with another person.

A Privacy Policy is not intended to replace such point-of-collection notices.

Where we rely on another lawful ground permitted by applicable law, we may process information without separate consent to the extent permitted by that law.

## 6. Withdrawal of consent

Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw that consent through available account controls or by contacting us.

Withdrawing consent does not make previous lawful processing unlawful.

Certain features may stop functioning if the personal data necessary to provide them can no longer be processed.

Where applicable, we will cease processing based on withdrawn consent and instruct relevant processors to do the same, except where continued processing is required or permitted by law.

## 7. How Doclyzer uses artificial intelligence

Doclyzer may use artificial-intelligence models to convert structured report information into simpler educational explanations.

The AI system is not asked to independently diagnose you.

Whenever technically feasible, our architecture is designed so that our own document-processing and validation systems identify report information before relevant structured data is supplied to the explanation model.

An AI-generated response may still be incomplete or inaccurate.

Doclyzer therefore does not guarantee that an AI explanation will be error-free.

AI explanations should always be read together with the original report.

Doclyzer may use business/API AI providers whose published terms state that API customer inputs and outputs are not used for model training by default. Doclyzer intends not to opt health-report data into voluntary model-training programmes.

We may change AI providers where necessary to improve security, availability, accuracy, cost or functionality, subject to this Privacy Policy and applicable law.

## 8. Medical and educational nature of the Service

**Doclyzer helps you organize and better understand your health reports. The information provided is for educational purposes only and is not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical guidance.**

Doclyzer does not diagnose diseases, prescribe medication, recommend treatment, replace a medical professional or determine whether you are medically fit or unfit.

Reference intervals can vary between laboratories, testing methods and individuals.

Where available, Doclyzer attempts to preserve the reference interval stated on the uploaded report instead of presenting a single range as universally applicable.

If Doclyzer or substantially all of the relevant business is involved in a merger, restructuring, acquisition or similar corporate transaction, information may be transferred subject to appropriate confidentiality, legal and data-protection requirements.

## 11. Data retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, for the period requested by you where the Service supports user-controlled storage, or for a longer period where required by law.

Medical reports and historical records are generally retained while your account or relevant profile remains active so that Doclyzer can provide report history and trends.

You may delete individual reports where the product provides that functionality.

If you delete your account, we will initiate deletion of personal information associated with the account, subject to lawful retention requirements, fraud-prevention needs, payment records, dispute-resolution requirements and limited backup cycles.

Certain technical, security and processing logs may be retained for periods required by applicable law.

Where applicable DPDP Rules require a minimum retention period for specified security or processing logs, we will comply with that requirement.

Information already disclosed to a recipient at your direction cannot always be deleted from that recipient's systems by Doclyzer.
