## 6. Automated Report Extraction and OCR Limitations

## 8. Reference Intervals Are Not Universal

Laboratory reference intervals may differ between laboratories, testing methods, equipment, units of measurement, age groups, biological sex, pregnancy status, geographic populations, and other clinical circumstances.

Where available, Doclyzer attempts to use the **reference interval stated on the uploaded report**.

A value shown as "Below reference interval", "Within reference interval", or "Above reference interval" is a description based on the information available from that report. It is **not a diagnosis**.

A result within a stated reference interval does not guarantee that no health problem exists, and a result outside a reference interval does not automatically mean that a disease is present.

Doclyzer may display test values over time and show whether a value has increased, decreased, or remained similar.

These trend indicators are intended to organize historical information.

An increase or decrease is **not automatically good or bad**. Medical significance may depend on the test, size of change, time between tests, laboratory methodology, medications, symptoms, medical history, and other clinical circumstances.

Historical charts and comparisons should not be interpreted as clinical recommendations.
