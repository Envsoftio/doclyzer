## 1. General No-Refund Policy

Doclyzer operates using a prepaid digital credit/token model.

**Except where a refund, reversal, replacement, or other remedy is required by applicable law or by the rules of the payment platform through which the purchase was made, all purchases of Doclyzer Credits/Tokens are final and non-refundable.**

Once a successful payment has been completed and the corresponding Credits/Tokens have been credited to your Doclyzer account, the purchase is considered delivered.

This applies whether the purchased Credits/Tokens are:

- fully consumed;
- partially consumed;
- completely unused;
- no longer required by the user;
- purchased by mistake;
- purchased in a larger quantity than required; or
- remaining in the account after the user decides to stop using Doclyzer.

Doclyzer does not provide cash refunds, bank transfers, reversals, or monetary redemptions merely because a user changes their mind after successfully purchasing Credits/Tokens.

Nothing in this Policy is intended to exclude or restrict any non-waivable right or remedy available under applicable law.

## 2. Why Token Purchases Are Non-Refundable

Doclyzer Credits/Tokens are digital units made available immediately after successful payment.

Once credited, they can be used to access digital functionality such as report processing, report analysis, AI-generated educational explanations, or other eligible features shown within Doclyzer.

Because Credits/Tokens are delivered digitally and become available for immediate use, they are generally not returnable in the same manner as physical goods.

Accordingly, users should review the following before completing payment:

- the price of the selected package;
- the number of Credits/Tokens included;
- any applicable taxes;
- the account receiving the Credits/Tokens;
- the payment method;
- the current credit cost of the intended feature; and
- any promotional terms shown before purchase.

Users should complete a purchase only after confirming that the selected package is appropriate for their needs.

## 3. Unused Credits/Tokens Are Not Refundable

Unused Credits/Tokens do not entitle the user to a cash refund.

For example, if a user purchases 150 Credits/Tokens and uses only 20, the monetary value attributable to the remaining 130 Credits/Tokens will not normally be refunded.

The remaining eligible Credits/Tokens may continue to be used within the Doclyzer account in accordance with the Terms and Conditions.

Unless otherwise expressly disclosed before purchase or required by applicable platform rules, purchased Credits/Tokens are intended not to expire solely because they remain unused.

---

## 4. Partially Used Credit Packs

Credit or token packs cannot normally be partially refunded.

If any portion of a purchased Credit/Token package has been consumed, Doclyzer will not refund the unused balance merely because the user no longer wishes to continue using the Service.

Credits/Tokens are not individually priced financial instruments and should not be interpreted as carrying a separately redeemable cash value.

## 5. Credits/Tokens Have No Cash Value

Doclyzer Credits/Tokens:

- are not legal tender;
- are not electronic money;
- are not a bank deposit;
- do not earn interest;
- cannot be withdrawn as cash;
- cannot normally be transferred to a bank account;
- cannot be exchanged for cryptocurrency or another monetary instrument;
- cannot be sold or resold unless Doclyzer expressly permits it; and
- may only be used for eligible Doclyzer services.

A displayed conversion such as "10 Credits = 1 report analysis" is a service-usage rule and does not create a cash-redemption value.

## 7. No Refund Based on Report Results

A user is not entitled to a refund merely because:

- all values in a report fall within the laboratory's stated reference intervals;
- a report contains fewer tests than expected;
- a result is not medically concerning;
- the explanation confirms information the user already knew;
- the user disagrees with an educational explanation;
- the user expected a diagnosis, treatment recommendation, prescription, or medical consultation; or
- the report does not produce the outcome or insight the user hoped to receive.

Doclyzer is an educational and report-management service. It does not guarantee a particular medical finding or outcome.

## 9. Failed Report Processing

If Credits/Tokens are deducted but Doclyzer is unable to complete the requested report-processing or analysis operation because of a confirmed technical failure on Doclyzer's side, our normal remedy will be one or more of the following, as appropriate:

1. retry the processing without an additional token charge;
2. restore the deducted Credits/Tokens to the user's Doclyzer balance; or
3. provide another equivalent in-service remedy.

A failed processing attempt does not automatically create a right to receive money back to the original payment method where the underlying purchased Credits/Tokens remain valid and available for use.

Where applicable law requires a monetary refund or another remedy, Doclyzer will comply with that requirement.

A refund is not ordinarily available where processing cannot be completed because the uploaded document:
